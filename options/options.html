How much Claude thinks before answering. Medium is plenty for most papers.

"Always" makes Paper Q&A Chrome's PDF viewer: PDFs open in it straight away, including local files when file access is allowed. Going Back from the viewer shows the PDF in Chrome's own viewer.

Clicking a citation, or the toolbar's paper info button, looks the paper up for its title, authors, venue, citation count, summary and links. Semantic Scholar, Crossref, OpenAlex and arXiv are asked at once and the first match is shown. Only the reference text, the paper's title or its identifiers (DOI, arXiv ID) are sent, and only when you point at or click a citation. Found papers are kept with the cached answers. With previews off, citations jump to the reference list as usual.

Without a key, Semantic Scholar's public rate limit is often used up, and lookups then use Crossref, OpenAlex and arXiv instead, which have no TL;DR summaries. A free key gives you your own limit: request one. It is stored only in this browser and sent only to api.semanticscholar.org.

To use local PDFs (file://), open chrome://extensions, click Details on Paper Q&A and turn on Allow access to file URLs.
